**Airport Housing Society** is one of Rawalpindi's best-known family neighbourhoods. Our own office is in **Gulistan Plaza, Sector 4**, so this guide comes from working in the society every day.

## Location & connectivity

The society sits near **Gulzar-e-Quaid** with easy access to the **Islamabad Expressway**, which makes it convenient for people who work in either Rawalpindi or Islamabad. Daily needs — markets, mosques, schools and clinics — are close by in most sectors.

## Sectors and property types

The society is divided into multiple sectors. You will find:

- **Houses** — mostly 5, 7, 8, 10 and 12 marla, single and double storey
- **Upper and lower portions** — very popular for rent
- **Residential plots** — for families who want to build their own home
- **Shops and plazas** on the main roads

Prices depend heavily on the **sector, street width, corner/park-facing location** and the **age of construction**.

## Buying in Airport Housing Society

1. Decide on size and budget (compare **price per marla**).
2. Shortlist sectors that suit your commute and schools.
3. Verify the file and dues at the society office — see our [documents checklist](/guides/property-documents-checklist-rawalpindi-islamabad/).
4. Inspect the house in daylight and check utility bills.

## Renting in Airport Housing Society

Rental demand is strong because of the location. Portions and full houses for families move quickly, so it helps to share your requirements early. Owners should expect tenant verification and a written rental agreement — we handle both.
