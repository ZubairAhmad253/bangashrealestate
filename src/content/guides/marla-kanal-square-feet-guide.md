If you are buying a plot or a house in Rawalpindi or Islamabad, you will hear sizes in **marla** and **kanal** rather than square feet. This guide explains what these units mean, why the same "5 marla" can be different sizes in different places, and how to convert them quickly.

## What is a marla?

A marla is the traditional unit of land area used in Pakistan and parts of India. The important thing to know is that **one marla does not have the same size everywhere**:

- **1 marla = 225 square feet** — this is the size used by most modern housing societies, including Bahria Town, DHA and many societies in Rawalpindi and Islamabad.
- **1 marla = 272.25 square feet** — the traditional (revenue / government) marla, still used in older land records and some areas.

Always ask which standard a society or seller is using before you compare prices.

## What is a kanal?

**1 kanal = 20 marla.** So depending on the marla standard:

| Unit | Using 225 sq ft marla | Using 272.25 sq ft marla |
|---|---|---|
| 1 marla | 225 sq ft | 272.25 sq ft |
| 5 marla | 1,125 sq ft | 1,361 sq ft |
| 10 marla | 2,250 sq ft | 2,723 sq ft |
| 1 kanal (20 marla) | 4,500 sq ft | 5,445 sq ft |

## Other common units

- **1 square yard (gaz) = 9 square feet**
- **1 square metre ≈ 10.764 square feet**
- **1 acre = 8 kanal = 160 marla** (traditional standard)

## Common plot sizes in Rawalpindi & Islamabad

In societies like **Airport Housing Society**, **Ghauri Town** and **Bahria Town**, you will mostly see 3, 5, 7, 10 marla and 1 kanal plots. A 5 marla plot is the most popular size for small families, while 10 marla and 1 kanal plots suit larger families and luxury homes.

## Covered area vs plot area

The plot size (e.g. 5 marla) is the **land** area. The **covered area** of a house built on it is usually larger, because most houses are double-storey. For example, a 5 marla (1,125 sq ft) double-storey house often has roughly 1,800–2,200 sq ft of covered area, depending on the design and society bylaws.

## Tips before you buy

1. Confirm whether the marla is 225 or 272.25 sq ft.
2. Check the exact dimensions on the society map (e.g. 25 × 45 ft).
3. Compare prices **per marla**, not just total price.
