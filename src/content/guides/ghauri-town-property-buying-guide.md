Ghauri Town sits along the Islamabad Expressway, on the Islamabad side of the Rawalpindi–Islamabad border, and for many families it is the first realistic step towards owning a home. Smaller houses, flats and plots in a wide range of sizes keep it within reach of buyers who are priced out of larger societies. That popularity also means a lot of variation in quality, so a careful buyer can find good value here while a rushed one can make expensive mistakes. This guide covers what we check when buying in Ghauri Town for our own clients.

## Who Ghauri Town suits

- **First-time buyers and young families** who want an affordable house rather than renting long term
- **Buyers who work in Rawalpindi or Islamabad** and want Expressway access to both
- **Small investors** looking at portions or flats for rental income
- **Plot buyers** who want to build a compact family home

If you need a large house with wide streets and a quieter setting, compare it with a few other societies before deciding. Our [Ghauri Town area page](/areas/ghauri-town/) gives a quick overview of the property types available here.

## Understand the phases first

Ghauri Town is made up of several phases and blocks, and they are not all the same. Some are densely built and fully settled; others are less developed. Street width, the quality of roads and drainage, and the mix of houses and shops can change from one block to the next.

This is why the phase and block matter as much as the size. A 5 marla house in one block may not be comparable with a 5 marla house in another. When you get a quote, always note the **phase, block and street width** and compare against similar properties.

## What you will find

- **Houses** — mostly smaller sizes, often double storey, both owner-built and builder-built
- **Portions** — upper and lower portions for families who want a smaller space
- **Flats** — in apartment buildings, mostly bought for rent or by small families
- **Plots** — for building your own house or holding
- **Shops** — on main roads and commercial streets

## Checks specific to Ghauri Town

### Approval and NOC

Approval status can differ between phases of the same society. Ask for the NOC or approval documents for the phase you are buying in, and check the current position with the development authority yourself or through your agent. Also confirm that the plot or house appears on the layout plan.

### Ownership paperwork

Find out early how the property is transferred. Some properties are transferred through a society file, while others go through **registry and mutation (intiqal)** in the land records. The documents, costs and checks are different in each case. Make sure the seller's name matches the title documents and their CNIC. Our [property documents checklist](/guides/property-documents-checklist-rawalpindi-islamabad/) covers what to ask for.

### Water, gas and electricity

Utilities are where many Expressway buyers get surprised. Ask:

- Does the house get water from society supply, its own boring, or tankers? How deep is the boring and is the water usable?
- Is there a legal gas connection, and is the meter in the current owner's name?
- Is the electricity meter legal, and are the latest bills paid?

Recent paid bills are the simplest proof that connections are legal and clear.

### Streets, drainage and access

Visit the street after heavy rain if you can. Low-lying streets and narrow lanes can collect water, and some inner streets are difficult for larger vehicles. Also check how you reach the Expressway from the property at peak times, because the route from an inner block can take longer than it looks on a map.

### Construction quality in ready houses

Many ready houses in Ghauri Town were built for sale rather than by owners for themselves. Quality ranges from good to poor. Check for:

- Dampness on walls, especially near the floor and in bathrooms
- Cracks in walls and ceilings, and signs of roof leakage
- Plumbing pressure and drainage in every bathroom and kitchen
- Wiring quality and the condition of the distribution board

If you are unsure, it is worth having a builder look at the structure before you pay a token.

### Plot measurements

Ask which marla standard is used and check the actual dimensions on site against the map. Our [marla calculator](/tools/marla-calculator/) helps you convert sizes into square feet so you can compare offers properly.

## Buying a flat in Ghauri Town

For flats, the building matters as much as the unit. Ask who manages the building and how much maintenance is charged, whether there is reliable water supply to upper floors, whether parking is available, and whether the building has the required approvals. Check the condition of stairs, the lift if there is one, and the common areas, because they reflect how well the building is run.
