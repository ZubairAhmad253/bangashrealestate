Buying property is one of the biggest financial decisions a family makes. In Rawalpindi and Islamabad, most problems happen because buyers pay a token **before** verifying documents. This checklist covers what to ask for and check before you commit.

## 1. Proof of ownership

- **Society file / allotment letter** (for housing societies like Airport Housing Society, Bahria Town or DHA), or
- **Registry / sale deed and fard** (for land outside societies).

Make sure the seller's name matches their **CNIC**. If someone is selling on behalf of the owner, ask for a valid **general power of attorney**.

## 2. Society verification

Visit the society office (or ask your agent to do it) and confirm:

- The plot or house number exists on the approved map
- The current owner on society records
- That there are **no outstanding dues** (development charges, maintenance, transfer fees)
- Whether the plot is **possession** or still a file

## 3. NOC and approvals

- **NOC of the society** from the relevant development authority
- **Approved building map** for constructed houses — check that the house matches the approved plan
- **Completion certificate** where applicable

## 4. Utility bills

Ask for recent **electricity, gas and water bills** to confirm there are no unpaid amounts and the connections are legal.

## 5. Physical inspection

- Measure the plot or check dimensions against the society map
- Check for encroachments and the condition of the structure
- Confirm the exact location — corner, park-facing or main boulevard plots carry different prices

## 6. Token and agreement

Only pay a token after verification, and always with a written **agreement to sell** that includes the price, payment schedule, transfer date and penalties if either party backs out.
